# Code of Conduct

Tasker is a small, privacy-focused community.

Be respectful, constructive, and clear. Disagreements are welcome; personal attacks are not.

Participants should:
- collaborate in good faith
- respect privacy and security concerns
- keep discussion focused on improving Tasker
- welcome new contributors

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, abuse, threats, doxxing, or repeated disruption.

Maintainers may remove contributions or participants that violate this policy.
